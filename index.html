About the firm

Legal support built around clarity, preparation, and self-advocacy.

Butler Legal Service is led by Shatrasha Butler, a solo attorney serving clients in Missouri and North Carolina. The firm was created for people and businesses who need legal guidance that feels direct, practical, and grounded.

The work is not about overwhelming clients with legal jargon. It is about helping them understand their position, prepare for important conversations, review options, and move forward with a stronger sense of control.
