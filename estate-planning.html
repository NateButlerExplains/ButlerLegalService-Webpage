Estate & Legacy Planning

Guidance for individuals and families who want to plan ahead, organize their affairs, and put their wishes in writing for the people who matter most.

How We Can Help

Estate planning is one of the clearest ways to prepare for the future and reduce uncertainty for the people you care about. Butler Legal Service offers consulting support for:

  • Wills and basic estate planning documents;
  • Understanding the options available for organizing your affairs and legacy;
  • Structured estate planning bundles offered through community and outreach events, including wills clinics; and
  • Preparing questions and gathering information ahead of a consultation.

What to Expect

Estate planning bundles and community programs typically begin with an interest form or questionnaire at a community or outreach event. Completing that form reserves your place in the intake process; it does not, by itself, mean the Firm has taken on your matter. Services begin only after your questionnaire is reviewed, any applicable participation requirements are met, and a written engagement agreement is signed.
