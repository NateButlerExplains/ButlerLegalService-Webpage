We Are Not a Full-Service Firm

Butler Legal Service, P.L.L.C. is not a full-service law firm. We concentrate on specific areas where we bring particular expertise and advocacy, and we do not attempt to serve every legal need. If your matter isn't something we handle, we'll tell you, and we may point you toward other resources — but we're not undertaking to advise you on it.

Licensed Only in Missouri and North Carolina

The attorney is licensed to practice law only in Missouri and North Carolina and does not intend to give advice to anyone on a legal matter that does not involve Missouri or North Carolina law. If your issue involves the law of another state, please seek an attorney licensed in that jurisdiction.

No Claim of Specialization

Nobody at Butler Legal Service, P.L.L.C. currently holds a board certification as a specialist in any particular area of law, and we do not hold ourselves out as such. Nothing on this Site should be read to suggest that we are "specialists" in any given area, including estate planning, civil rights, or higher education law. North Carolina attorneys may not use "specialist," "specialize," or similar variants in describing their services except as permitted under Rule 7.4 of the North Carolina Rules of Professional Conduct, and we adhere to that restriction.

Community Outreach and Intake Materials

Content shared at community events, church partnerships, or through informational flyers, including anything about estate planning bundles is for general education. Completing an interest form or questionnaire at one of these events starts the intake process; it is not, by itself, an engagement of the Firm. Representation begins only once we've confirmed we can take your matter, cleared any conflicts, and both signed a written agreement.

Confidentiality of Correspondence

If you email us before an attorney-client relationship is established, please avoid sharing sensitive details about your legal matter. Any outcomes, results, or examples referenced on this Site (if any) are specific to the facts of those matters. Past results do not guarantee a similar outcome in any future matter.

In Short

Nothing on this Site is legal advice. We're not your lawyers unless we both agree to that, in writing. If you have a legal question, the safest step is to reach out and ask whether we can help, not to rely on this website as a stand-in for that conversation.
